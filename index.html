Biography

Ajeet Kumar received his PhD from the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee in 2019, specialising in RF and Microwave Engineering. He was supported by the ISRO RISAT-1 fellowship from 2014 to 2017 and later contributed to the Chandrayaan-2 project as a postdoctoral researcher. He joined CNIT RaSS in February 2021 and was made permanent in October 2023. He currently serves as Technical Lead on the ESA EO4Security project and works broadly on polarimetric and hybrid-polarimetric SAR, interferometric SAR, and inverse SAR. He is a URSI Senior Member and has received the URSI Young Scientist Award twice, in 2022 and 2024. He also serves as Secretary of the IEEE P3355 SAR Working Group.
