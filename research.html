Research themes

Research

Current work focuses on advancing radar imaging and remote sensing through strong methodological foundations in polarimetry, inverse SAR, and data-driven analysis.

Polarimetric and hybrid-polarimetric SAR

Methodological work on scattering decomposition, hybrid-pol parameter estimation, and sensor-aware interpretation for remote sensing.

Three-Dimensional Polarimetric InISAR Imaging of Non-Cooperative Targets

Inverse SAR and 3D Pol-InISAR imaging of non-cooperative targets

Development of reconstruction pipelines for 3D imaging, target recognition, and multi-aspect inverse SAR analysis.

Three-Dimensional Polarimetric InISAR Imaging

Lunar SAR remote sensing

Use of RISAT-1 and Chandrayaan-2 data to develop lunar surface and regolith analysis tools using radar backscatter and dielectric estimation.

Lunar SAR and dielectric estimation work

SAR for environmental and security applications

Applications in oil-spill monitoring, environmental surveillance, and the ESA EO4Security project.

Application of Hybrid-Pol SAR in Oil-Spill Detection

Machine learning for SAR target recognition

Research on transformer-based methods and interpretable deep learning for SAR-based target recognition and classification tasks.

Transformer-Based Automatic Target Recognition for 3D-InISAR
